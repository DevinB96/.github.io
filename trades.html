How it works

Start small. Prove it. Then grow it.

We start with a short audit of how enquiries, quotes and jobs move through your business today, then build the one or two workflows that will make the biggest difference first.

1. Audit, 2–3 weeks

We sit with you (and whoever runs the office), map the journey from enquiry to paid invoice, and show you where time and jobs are leaking.

2. Build, a few weeks

We set up the workflows in your own accounts, test them on real enquiries, and tune the wording until it sounds like you.

3. Run and improve

You use it day to day. We check the numbers with you and add the next workflow when you’re ready, or hand it over completely. You get a live results dashboard showing what’s changed.

Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else, ask us on the call.

01I’m on site all day. Do I need to sit at a computer?

No. Approvals come to your phone as a message or email you can approve with one tap, edit, or ignore. The point is to give you time back, not add another screen.

02Will my customers know it’s AI?

Messages are written in your voice and approved by you, so they’re your messages. If a customer asks, we’d always recommend being honest that you use tools to help you reply quickly.

03We’re a small firm. Is this worth it?

Often it matters most for small firms, because there’s no office team to pick up the slack. If one or two extra jobs a quarter would pay for it, it usually does. If it wouldn’t, we’ll tell you.

04What about my customers’ data?

Everything runs in accounts you own, on business plans with proper privacy terms, and only the details each step needs are used. We follow UK GDPR and put a simple data agreement in place with you.

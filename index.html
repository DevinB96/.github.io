Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else, ask us on the call.

01I’m on site all day. Do I need to sit at a computer?

No. Approvals come to your phone as a message you can approve with one tap, edit or ignore. The whole point is to give you time back, not add another screen.

02Will customers know it’s automated?

Messages are written in your words and approved by you, so they’re your messages. They just go out faster. If a customer asks, we’d always recommend being honest that you use tools to help you reply quickly.

03Do I need a new website?

Usually not. The package works with the website, phone number and Google profile you already have. If your site has no enquiry form, we’ll add a simple one as part of the set-up.

04We’re a small firm. Is it worth it?

It often matters most for small firms, because there’s no office team to pick up the phone. If one or two extra jobs a quarter would pay for it, it usually does. If they wouldn’t, we’ll tell you.

05Am I tied into a contract?

No. The set-up is a one-off fee. Monthly care can be cancelled with 30 days’ notice, and everything we built stays in your accounts.

06What about my customers’ data?

Everything runs in accounts you own, on business plans with proper privacy terms, and only the details each step needs are used. We follow UK GDPR and put a simple data agreement in place with you.

      Questions

      Straight answers.

      If yours isn’t here, ask. We’d rather tell you AI isn’t the right fix than sell you something that isn’t.

      01Do we need technical people on our team?

      No. We handle the design, build and integration. Your team learns to use and adjust the systems in plain English, and we leave behind documentation written for non-technical people.

      02Which AI tools do you use?

      Whatever fits. We’re vendor-neutral and work with leading AI models and automation platforms, connected to the tools you already rely on: email, CRM, accounting, calendars and messaging. We recommend based on your stack, budget and data, not on commissions.

      03Is our data safe?

      Data handling is part of every design from day one. We review what data each workflow touches, prefer business-grade services with clear privacy terms, limit access to what’s needed, and keep a human approval step on anything sensitive or customer-facing.

      04How quickly will we see results?

      Most roadmaps include quick wins that can go live within the first few weeks. Larger, connected systems are built and piloted over a sprint. We agree on the measures of success before any build starts.

      05What does it cost?

      Every engagement is scoped and priced up front, so there are no surprises. The audit is the smallest commitment and often pays for itself by showing exactly where to invest. Tell us about your business and we’ll give you a clear proposal.

      06Will AI replace our staff?

      That’s not our aim. We take repetitive work off people’s plates so they can spend more time with customers, on sales and on the judgement calls only humans can make.

How we handle your information

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information Brooks Process Solutions collects through this website, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and your rights under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). In short: we only use your details to reply to you and to work with you, we never sell them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Who we are

Brooks Process Solutions is an AI implementation consultancy run by Devin Brooks and based in Guildford, Surrey. We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact: brooksprocesssolutions@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company, team size, the areas you’d like help with, and your message.
  • Booking requests: your name, email, company, the time you requested and any notes you add.
  • Website tools: the calculator, readiness check and use-case shortlist run in your browser. Their results are only sent to us if you choose to include them in a message or booking.

We don’t use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies on this website.

International transfers

Some of these services are based in, or store data in, countries outside the UK, including the United States. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards UK law allows, such as UK adequacy regulations (including the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where a provider is certified) or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

How long we keep it

If you become a client, we keep your details for as long as we work together, and business records for as long as the law requires afterwards (usually six years). If you don’t, we delete your enquiry within 24 months of our last contact, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or complete it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it, including any marketing;
  • ask us to transfer it to you or another organisation, where that applies.

To use any of these rights, email brooksprocesssolutions@gmail.com. We’ll respond within one month. There’s normally no charge.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Marketing emails

If we email you about our services, every email says who we are and includes an easy way to opt out. If you opt out, we’ll stop straight away and keep only the minimum needed to make sure we don’t contact you again.

Security

We keep access to your information limited to the people and services that need it, use accounts protected with strong passwords and two-step verification, and never copy client data into personal tools.

Children

This website and our services are for businesses. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

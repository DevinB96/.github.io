How we handle your information

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Brooks Process Solutions (“we”, “us”) is an AI implementation consultancy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. In short: we only use your details to reply to you and to work with you, we never sell them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company, team size, the areas you’d like help with, and your message.
  • Booking requests: your name, email, company, the time you requested and any notes you add.
  • Website tools: the calculator, readiness check and use-case shortlist run in your browser. Their results are only sent to us if you choose to include them in a message or booking.

We don’t use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange any call you request.
  • To prepare for and follow up on our conversations, and to send proposals you ask for.
  • To keep a simple record of our contact with you, so nothing falls through the cracks.

We use AI tools to help organise enquiries and draft replies. A person reviews every reply before it is sent, and nothing is decided about you automatically.

How long we keep it

If you become a client, we keep your details for as long as we work together and as long as our records require afterwards. If you don’t, we delete your enquiry within 24 months of our last contact, or sooner if you ask.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email brooksprocesssolutions@gmail.com and we’ll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law, and the right to complain to your data protection regulator.

Security

We keep access to your information limited to the people and services that need it, use accounts protected with strong passwords and two-step verification, and never copy client data into personal tools.

Children

This website and our services are for businesses. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

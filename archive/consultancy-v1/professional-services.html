What we set up

Five workflows that give fee-earners their time back.

Each one runs inside the systems you already use and works within your professional obligations. AI prepares, people decide, and nothing reaches a client without review.

01

Enquiry triage and booking

You stay in control: you decide who to take on; AI only sorts and drafts.

Today

Website and email enquiries sit in a shared inbox until someone senior has time to read them.

With AI

Each enquiry is summarised, tagged by service and urgency, and given a drafted reply with consultation slots, ready for a quick check.

02

Client onboarding

You stay in control: regulated checks such as ID and AML stay with your team and your approved providers.

Today

Checklists, document requests and reminders are managed by hand for every new client.

With AI

A welcome pack, document checklist and polite reminders go out automatically, and you can see at a glance what’s still missing.

03

Meeting notes to follow-up

You stay in control: recording only with consent, and you edit before anything is sent.

Today

Notes are typed up later, and actions and follow-ups depend on memory.

With AI

Minutes after the meeting: a summary, a list of actions with owners and a draft follow-up email to the client, filed against the matter or project.

04

Proposals and fee letters

You stay in control: you set the scope and fees; AI assembles the draft.

Today

Each proposal is rebuilt from an old one, with the risk of carrying over the wrong details.

With AI

A first draft is built from your approved templates and clauses, using details from the enquiry and the call, ready for review in minutes.

05

Your firm’s know-how, on tap

You stay in control: it only uses documents you choose, with access limited to your team.

Today

Juniors interrupt seniors for answers that already exist somewhere in the files.

With AI

A private assistant answers “how do we usually…” questions from your own procedures, precedents and FAQs, and shows where each answer came from.

01Is it safe to use AI with client information?

It can be, if it’s set up properly. We use business-grade services with contractual data protections, keep data in your own accounts, limit what each step can see, and document it all so you can show your regulator and insurer how it works.

02Will it give advice to our clients?

No. The workflows handle admin, drafting and organisation. Professional advice stays with your qualified people, and nothing reaches a client without review.

03We already have a practice management system. Do we have to replace it?

No. We build around the systems you already have and connect to them where they allow it. Our job is to make your current tools work harder, not to sell you new ones.

04Where do we start?

With a short audit: we map how enquiries, onboarding and client work move through the firm, then build the one or two workflows that will save the most time first. You see results before committing to more, tracked on a live results dashboard.
